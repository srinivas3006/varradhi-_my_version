[ ] Run the list, detail and vote flows as a guest and as a signed-in reader.

    different saved location receives the account's eligible polls.
    Repeat with notifications disabled on a valid unowned installation.
    An absent/unknown guest credential yields only global polls. A wrong
    secret for a known installation must trigger the existing credential

[ ] Open a poll with at least three options. Compare the visible order with
